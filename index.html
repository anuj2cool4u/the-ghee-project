Our Story

Made in Sydney,
for kitchens that care.

The Ghee Project started the way most good things do — around a kitchen table, frustrated by what was on supermarket shelves. Additives. Preservatives. Bulk import product dressed up with a nice label.

We set out to make ghee the way it has always been made: pure clarified butter, slowly rendered, from dairy you can trace back to NSW farms. Nothing added. Nothing hidden.

Today we produce in small batches from our Sydney commercial kitchen — keeping every jar at the quality we'd serve at our own tables.

The Process

Slow, careful, deliberate.

Every batch follows the same four steps — because the quality of ghee lives in the details.

01

Source

We work directly with NSW dairy farms to source the best quality unsalted butter we can find. Provenance matters.

02

Render

Butter is slow-rendered at a controlled temperature — long enough to remove all the water and milk solids, without burning the fat.

03

Infuse

For flavoured variants, whole spices and fresh aromatics are added at the right moment in the render — not after. The oil does the work.

04

Jar

Each batch is filtered and filled to order from our Sydney kitchen. No warehouse. No months on a shelf before it reaches you.
